Digital Business Setup

E-Commerce License & Website Setup in Qatar

Launch your online store legally with MOCI approved e-commerce activities and domain registration.

Domain & Website Regulations

  • Authorized Extensions: Only .qa and .com website domain extensions are permitted for official e-commerce licensing in Qatar[cite: 95, 96].
  • Approved Registrars: Domains must be registered through authorized registrars such as Ooredoo, Routedge, Abu-Ghazaleh, Sadad, Social Tech, Qhost, Badges, or WAFRA IT[cite: 95, 96].
  • Estimated Cost: Complete e-commerce setup including CR, licensing, and professional website integration starts from approximately 3,500 QAR.

MOCI Activities & Scope

  • 194 Specific Activities: There are precisely 194 approved commercial activities under MOCI guidelines designated specifically for opening an e-commerce business.
  • Single Website Rule: Each e-commerce license is tied to its specific registered domain. To operate a secondary online store, a separate license request must be initiated[cite: 95].
  • End-to-End Execution: Our team assists with activity selection, domain validation, gateway setup, and Ministry single window submission.
