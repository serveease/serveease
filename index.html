Step-by-Step Execution

End-to-End Business Setup Process (8 Easy Steps)

From initial consultation to CR issuance, office lease, and labor setup

1. Initial Consultation

Analysis of business concept, structure selection (WLL/Single Owner), and MOCI activity alignment.

2. Document Collection

Collecting QIDs, passports, trade names, capital details, and national address certificates.

3. Project Report Prep

Drafting application details covering shareholders, Ultimate Beneficial Owner, and capital structure.

4. Application & Approvals

CR Approval, Trade Name reservation, AOA signing at Ministry of Justice (3-7 working days).

5. Payment & CR Issuance

Issuance of CR, Labor Confirmation, Trade Name Certificate, Tax Card, and Computer Card.

6. Office & Commercial License

Securing commercial lease, Baladiya municipal license approval, and office signboard compliance.

7. Stamp & Letterhead

Creating official company stamp and branded corporate letterheads for banking and official use.

8. Immigration & Labor

Setting up Labor Department portal, initiating work visas, residence permits, and employment contracts.
